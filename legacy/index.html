By the cabin, or the whole boat

Two ways to sail with us, and the only real difference is who else is at dinner.

Open trip

A scheduled departure. You reserve one cabin and share the boat with eight to twenty others.

  • Priced per person, per cabin grade
  • Fixed dates and a published route
  • 25% deposit, balance 60 days out
  • No single supplement on solo berths
See departures

Private charter

The whole boat, your dates, and an itinerary we draw with you rather than hand to you.

  • Priced per boat, per day
  • Any dates the boat is free
  • Route built around your group
  • A quote from a human within one working day

Journal

Written on the boat, mostly

By the crew, the cook, the captain and the biologist who joins four crossings a season. Nobody here is a content producer.

Read the journal
Safety
Oxygen and first-response kits on every deck, two liferafts, EPIRB and satellite phone. Crew drilled monthly, not annually.
Diving cover
DAN-affiliated evacuation cover on all four boats. We ask for your policy number with the joining form, and we do check it.
Booking terms
Deposit fully refundable to 90 days. One free move to another departure in the same season, whatever the notice. Read the policy.
A person, not a bot
The office is two people in Labuan Bajo. They answer on WhatsApp within a few hours, and it is genuinely them. Contact us.

Four boats, one family, and the eastern half of Indonesia.

We started with one phinisi built in Bira and a loan nobody sensible would have given us. The boats have multiplied. The crew list has barely changed.

The familia letter
